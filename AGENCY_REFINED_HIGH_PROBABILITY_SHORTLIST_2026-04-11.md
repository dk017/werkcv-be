# Agency Refined High-Probability Shortlist

These are the strongest official-site-sourced B2B leads for WerkCV's current offer:

`Dutch-first candidate-CV workflow for recruiters, detacheerders and outplacement firms that want consistent, ATS-proof output without manual Word formatting.`

## Tier 1: send first

1. Bridgewell
- Segment: executive search / interim boutique
- Why first: boutique, senior-leadership focus, public contact route, likely partner-led operations
- Angle: "van bron-CV naar rustige, client-ready leadership profile"
- Site: https://www.bridgewell.nl/en/

2. &deBlauw
- Segment: executive search boutique
- Why first: explicit boutique positioning, named managing partner, private-equity / family-business context where shortlist quality matters
- Angle: "consistente shortlist-output voor partner-led searches"
- Site: https://endeblauw.com/en/executive-search/

3. Apollo Executive Search
- Segment: executive search for investors and founders
- Why first: direct managing-partner contact, small-team feel, high-value founder / investor searches
- Angle: "sneller van intake naar investor/client-ready kandidaatprofiel"
- Site: https://apolloexecutivesearch.com/contact/

4. e-search
- Segment: boutique executive search
- Why first: founder-led, small-team signal, PE / VC / family-business niche
- Angle: "vervang handmatige CV-opschoning in partner-led searchwerk"
- Site: https://www.e-search.nl/

5. Search & Change
- Segment: finance executive search / interim CFO
- Why first: narrow niche, high shortlist expectations, direct relationship-led positioning
- Angle: "strakke CFO- en finance-profielen zonder Word-frictie"
- Site: https://searchandchange.nl/en/why-about/

## Tier 2: strong next wave

6. DUX
- Segment: executive search non-profit / culture
- Why next: official site plus vacancy flow still ask for CV + motivation, suggesting a live document workflow
- Angle: "consistente kandidaatdocumenten voor search in non-profit en cultuur"
- Site: https://dux.nl/en/

7. Cooster Executive Search
- Segment: management and directieposities MKB / private equity
- Why next: site explicitly lays out search/shortlist process, so presentation pain is plausible
- Angle: "kandidaatpresentatie standaardiseren richting MKB- en PE-opdrachtgevers"
- Site: https://www.coosterexecutive.nl/

8. Alice Pley Search
- Segment: executive search & interim management in marketing / communications
- Why next: niche specialist, founder-direct contact, very high-touch workflow
- Angle: "founder-to-founder pitch around consistent board and senior-advisory candidate output"
- Site: https://www.alicepleysearch.nl/en/contact/

## Tier 3: adjacent but worth testing

9. The Headhunter Group
- Segment: finance / investment management executive search
- Why test: niche and direct contact, but slightly more polished / established brand than ideal
- Site: https://theheadhuntergroup.com/

10. ASBR
- Segment: outplacement
- Why test: official outplacement page explicitly includes resume drafting and application coaching
- Site: https://www.asbr.nl/en/outplacement/

11. Dynamisch Bureau
- Segment: outplacement / reintegration
- Why test: outplacement angle is close to the problem, but less recruiter-like than the core wedge
- Site: https://www.dynamischbureau.nl/en/outplacement/

12. Carrierepoort
- Segment: outplacement / career transition
- Why test: likely recurring candidate volume, but less founder-friendly than the boutiques above
- Site: https://carrierepoort.nl/outplacementbureau/

## Not the first wave

These are relevant but not first-wave priorities:

- Undutchables
- Adams Multilingual Recruitment
- International Recruiters
- Staatsen Executive Search
- @reintegratie / AT Arbo

Reason: still plausible, but either broader, more process-heavy, or less clearly tied to the Word-to-client-CV workflow pain than the top 8.
