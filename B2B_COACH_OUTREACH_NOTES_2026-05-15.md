# B2B Coach Outreach Batch - 2026-05-15

Goal: send 30 Dutch first-touch emails to coach/outplacement/re-integratie/sollicitatiebegeleiding targets that likely touch candidate CVs directly.

Positioning for this batch:

- Do not mention price.
- Do not pitch the €149 plan in the first email.
- Ask how they currently handle CV production: feedback only, or do they turn messy work history/intake into a polished CV?
- Build trust by referencing the exact page/service.
- Plain text only.
- No hard demo ask.
- The intended reply is process discovery: "we do this in Word", "we only review", "not relevant", or "show me what you mean".

Why this is different from the earlier agency batch:

- Earlier copy leaned too much toward "pilot", "branded templates", and "15-minute call".
- This batch leads with a practical research question and avoids pricing.
- The list is weighted toward explicit CV/LinkedIn/sollicitatie/outplacement/re-integratie services, not generic coaches.
