# Agency 15-Day Close Plan

Window: `April 16, 2026` to `April 30, 2026`

Primary goal:

- close `1 paying recurring agency pilot` before `April 30, 2026`

What counts as a win:

- invoice-approved or verbally agreed `EUR249+/month` pilot
- clear start date
- one real workflow owner on the buyer side

What does **not** count as the main win:

- vague interest
- "send more info"
- unpaid curiosity
- a one-off B2C-like request with no recurring path

## The strategy

Your best path is not breadth. It is:

1. hit `6-8` highly qualified firms hard
2. get `2-3` live conversations
3. push one of them into:
   - sample on real material
   - then monthly pilot

Do not spend the next 15 days polishing product edges that do not directly help:

- replies
- calls
- sample turnaround
- paid pilot close

## Offer stack for this sprint

You only need two offers in practice:

### Offer A: recurring pilot

- `EUR249/month`
- `1` branded route
- founder-led onboarding
- fair-use framing
- privacy / retention agreements handled manually

Use this when:

- the pain is recurring
- they process multiple candidate profiles per month
- the buyer sounds operationally annoyed by current Word work

### Offer B: wedge into recurring

- free sample on `1` anonymized source CV or intake example

Use this only to unlock the conversation, not as the end state.

The flow is:

- outreach
- sample request
- sample returned fast
- short call
- close monthly pilot

Do **not** default to a tiny paid one-off unless they clearly refuse a monthly pilot. You want recurring revenue, not a collection of micro-custom jobs.

## Response rules

### If someone replies with interest

- answer same day
- propose `2` concrete call slots
- do not send a deck first unless they insist

### If someone asks for more info

Send:

- one-liner
- agency page link
- ask for a short call or sample input

Do not send a long essay.

### If someone says "we already do this in Word"

Use:

`Precies daarom dacht ik aan jullie. De pilot is alleen relevant als dat handmatige stuk vaak genoeg terugkomt en onnodig tijd kost.`

### If someone says "we don't want a big system"

Use:

`Dat is ook niet wat dit is. Dit is juist de kleine route tussen handwerk en een zwaar platformproject.`

### If someone says "we need proof first"

Use:

`Logisch. Daarom werk ik nu met een smalle sample op echt bronmateriaal en pas daarna pas een kleine maandpilot als het probleem terugkerend blijkt.`

## Close script

When a prospect is warm, the close should sound like this:

`Op basis van wat je beschrijft klinkt dit niet als een incidenteel documentprobleem, maar als terugkerend workflowwerk. Dan lijkt een kleine maandpilot logischer dan losse voorbeelden. Ik stel voor: we starten met 1 route, duidelijke afspraken over output en retentie, en kijken na de eerste maand of dit genoeg terugkomt om door te zetten.`

## Risks to avoid

- building new product features to avoid selling
- letting free samples become endless unpaid consulting
- talking to consultants who feel the pain but cannot sponsor the pilot
- accepting "interesting" as pipeline progress
- chasing low-fit outplacement enterprises before boutique search firms
