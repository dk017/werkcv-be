## Monday Rule

Do not personalize all 14 at once.

Do this instead:

1. Send the first 5 before lunch.
2. Review replies and objections.
3. Tighten the pitch once.
4. Send the next 5 with the improved version.
5. Leave the last 4 for Tuesday unless Monday goes unusually fast.
