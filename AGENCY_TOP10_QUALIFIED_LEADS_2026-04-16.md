# Agency Top 10 Qualified Leads

Checked against currently accessible official sites on `2026-04-16`.

Qualification logic used:

- boutique or niche firm rather than broad enterprise HR services
- executive search, interim, staffing, or adjacent outplacement workflow
- strong chance of recurring candidate-document work
- public contact route available now
- closeable with a founder-led pilot rather than a heavy procurement cycle
