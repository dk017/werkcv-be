# Consolidated Target Keywords

Date: `2026-04-23`

This file merges:

- Bing Webmaster keyword exports
- the extra Gemini keyword angles
- current WerkCV site coverage

Only keywords that truly fit the product are kept.

## Core Conclusion

Most of the useful opportunities fall into 5 buckets:

- core builder intent
- CV analysis / ATS intent
- calculator intent
- problem-solution articles
- comparison / alternative intent

Many Gemini ideas were directionally good, but not all of them are worth turning into pages.

## What Truly Makes Sense

### 1. Strongest commercial keywords

These are closest to the product and worth defending or building around first.

- `cv maken`
- `cv maker`
- `cv maken gratis`
- `gratis cv maken`
- `cv template`
- `cv voorbeeld`
- `cv format`
- `gratis cv check`
- `cv check`
- `cv beoordeling`
- `cv score berekenen`
- `transitievergoeding berekenen`
- `ontslagvergoeding berekenen`

### 2. Strong problem-solution keywords

These are high intent because the user already feels stuck.

- `waarom wordt mijn cv afgewezen`
- `cv afgewezen door ats`
- `cv checken op fouten`
- `cv door ai laten checken`
- `cv trefwoorden finder`
- `trefwoorden scanner cv`
- `zzp naar loondienst cv`

These are often better for articles, tool landings, or tool-support pages than for generic marketing pages.

### 3. Good comparison / alternative keywords

These fit WerkCV if they are handled through comparison pages, not broad builder pages.

- `canva cv`
- `europass cv`
- `cv.nl`
- `cvster`
- `cvmaker`

These are already mostly covered by the current comparison and opzeggen cluster.

### 4. Good expat keywords, but not first priority

These make sense, but many are already covered in the English / expat content cluster.

- `english cv for dutch job market`
- `dutch cv vs international resume`
- `cv format netherlands for foreigners`
- `working in netherlands without speaking dutch cv`
- `orientation year visa cv tips`
- `beste cv maker voor expats`

These are valid, but they are not the highest-leverage next build if the goal is to reach more daily B2C sales fast.

## What Does Not Make Sense

These do not justify separate page builds right now.

- Reddit search prompts like `recruiter ervaringen`, `loopbaanadvies`, `wie wil mijn cv checken`
- vague trend phrases like `ai bestendig cv` and `cv algoritme proof maken`
- service-implying terms like `cv feedback ontvangen` or `cv laten nakijken` unless you want to offer manual review
- noisy or irrelevant Bing terms like `feenstra cv onderhoud`, `cv ketel`, `c.v.`

## Where Gemini Was Right

Gemini was directionally right about these:

- CV analysis / ATS can be a major top-of-funnel to paid flow
- problem-solution keywords convert better than broad advice
- expat / Netherlands-format keywords are valuable
- comparison and competitor keywords are worth owning

## Where Gemini Was Too Broad

Gemini mixed together:

- SEO keywords
- product ideas
- Reddit outreach tactics
- paid ads ideas
- social proof advice

That is useful for strategy, but not all of it belongs in the keyword plan.

For the keyword plan, the best move is to keep only terms that:

- map clearly to a page or tool
- fit actual WerkCV intent
- can lead naturally into CV creation or paid PDF download

## Working Rule

Do not build a page just because the keyword sounds clever.

Build only if:

- the keyword maps to a real page type
- the page can genuinely help the visitor
- the CTA into WerkCV is natural
- the keyword does not force a misleading promise
