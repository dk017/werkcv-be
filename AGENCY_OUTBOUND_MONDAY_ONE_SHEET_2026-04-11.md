## Monday rules

- Send plain text only.
- Do not over-edit between emails.
- Send the first 5 before lunch.
- Review replies once.
- Tighten only once if needed.
- Send the next 5 after lunch.
- Ask for a reply or for 1 example, not for a demo.
- One link per email.

## Core line to remember

WerkCV helpt bureaus van bron-CV, intake of losse kandidaatinfo naar een rustig, consistent en ATS-proof Nederlands kandidaat-CV zonder terugkerend handmatig Word-werk.
